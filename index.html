A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

How Articulator works

01

Begin anywhere

Drop in a question, sentence, topic, or unfinished thought. Choose whether to deepen it, broaden its scope, or reframe it.

02

Follow meaning

The free Datamuse word API supplies WordNet-style links: synonyms, broader ideas, specific examples, and associated concepts. This is a lexical graph, not a strict WordNet lookup.

03

Choose your connections

Turn on the optional MiniLM embedding model to rank candidate links by semantic similarity. The quantized model downloads to your browser once; your text is embedded on-device.

04

Build a better starting prompt

When you choose Expand, topic words are looked up through Datamuse and your full idea plus selected links are sent to a free hosted model through Puter.js. If that service is unavailable, a useful local draft is still created. Saved ideas stay in this browser.

Your choice, every time. No API key is required. The AI is optional, and no idea is sent to it before you ask.
